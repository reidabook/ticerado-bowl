The Round Up · Week 6

State of the League

The league is bunched together. Six teams are at 4-2, five are at 3-3, and the all-play standings suggest Paul, Andy and Daniel are the strongest of the group, with the rest more dependent on schedule luck.

Luck is shaping the table. George and Nick are winning more than their scoring says they should, while Tex is 1-5 with a scoring average of 107.15, which is nearly the same as Joe's and Medvene's.

The injury list is a problem to track. Reid has multiple receivers and running backs dinged, Eamonn is missing DJ Moore, and Joe, George and Ian each have a starter to watch before kickoff.

Watch Paul against The Median, Andy against Medvene, and whether Joe's big week was a fluke. If Eamonn can't beat Joe, the bottom of the table starts to look settled.
