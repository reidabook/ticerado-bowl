Juggernauts, Jitters, and Jefferson

Three weeks in, the standings are a traffic jam: eight teams sit at 2-1, and the all-play numbers say only a couple of them deserve it. The Round Up · Week 3

State of the League

The league has a clear top tier: Daniel and Andy each have 30-6 all-play records, Paul is next at 23-13, and everyone else is bunched behind. Records don't show it yet, but the scoring does.

Luck is spread unevenly. Tex is 0-3 with a 9-27 all-play mark and a one-point loss to The Median, while Joe and Alex are 2-1 with below-average all-play numbers. Expect some of that to even out.

Injury watch: Medvene is the headline with Achane on IR and Jefferson questionable. Ian, George, Joe and Reid also have questionable starters, so check Sunday inactives.

What to watch: Nick vs. Paul is the marquee game, Tex gets Andy, and Reid vs. Joe is basically a coin flip. Greg gets The Median, which is about as friendly a matchup as this league offers.
