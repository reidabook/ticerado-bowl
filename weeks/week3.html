State of the League

The league has a clear top tier. Daniel and Andy are both 30-6 in all-play and scoring well above everyone else. Paul is next at 23-13, and then there is a large gap to the rest of the field.

The records don't match the scoring in several places. Six teams are 2-1, but Nick, Joe and Alex have gotten more out of their records than their point totals justify. Medvene is 1-2 despite the league's lowest scoring, and Tex is 0-3 with a middling scoring profile.

Injuries will matter this week. Medvene has Achane on injured reserve in a starting slot, Joe has Josh Allen questionable, and Reid has Breece Hall and Mike Evans both questionable. Waddle and Coker are also questionable in Ian and George's game.

Watch Nick vs. Paul for a sign of which 2-1 team is real, and check whether Tex can finally get a win against Andy. Also see whether Daniel's Flutes can keep pace against Eamonn.
