Flutes Blow the Doors Off

Daniel's Flutes were the class of Week 3, hanging 172.2 on George and winning by 73.7. Bijan Robinson supplied 35.0 of it. That's the top score of the week, the weekly payout, and the biggest blowout, all in one afternoon.

Andy's Giggle Island and Paul's Romo both stretched winning streaks to two. Andy got 33.6 from Brock Purdy to beat Ian 144.4 to 84.4. Paul rode Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 30.2 past Eamonn. Reid's Gibbs got 37.5 from Jahmyr Gibbs, the top individual score of the week, and beat Alex by 44.2.

At the bottom, Medvene scored 65.9 and lost to Nick, who won with just 102.4, exactly the median. Tex lost to The Median 102.4 to 101.3 and is 0-3. Eamonn left Tyler Shough (26.2) on the bench for Drake Maye (2.6), and his week went the way you'd expect.
